The Bar

A real neighborhood bar, not a theme of one

Locally owned and run. Pool inside, a big covered patio outside, string lights, a Texas flag on the wall and a bartender who'll remember what you drink.

  • Beer bar with set-ups. Ice cold domestics and imports. Bring your own bottle and we'll pour the mixers.
  • Cash only. Old school on purpose. Bring cash.
  • Pool tables and tournaments. Cheap games, weekly 8-ball, and regulars who'll take your money nicely.
  • Live music on the patio. Open mic, local bands and karaoke under the lights.
  • 21 and up. Sorry kids, this one's for the grown-ups.
